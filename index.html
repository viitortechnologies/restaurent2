Our Story

Happy Food brings you the authentic taste of Hyderabad, the city of pearls and biryani. Our recipes have been passed down through generations, preserving the rich culinary heritage of the Nizams.

From the aromatic Hyderabadi Biryani to the spicy Mirchi ka Salan, every dish tells a story of tradition, passion, and love for food that has made Hyderabad famous worldwide.
